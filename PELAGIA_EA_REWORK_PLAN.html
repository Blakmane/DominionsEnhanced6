EA Pelagia (Pearl Kings) — As-Built Summary

Target: #selectnation 40 in DomEnhanced2_GitHub.dm, branch pelagia-rework. This documents what the rework actually does, after the review feedback. Every unit/armour/weapon ID was verified against game data (dom6inspector CSVs + the per-nation recruit tables) and the mod's own definitions.

Architecture. Edits target EA's IDs only. The two indie-shared units (Turtle Tribe Triton 2382, Shark Tribe Triton 2383) are cloned to new IDs (13572, 13573) so wild independents keep vanilla stats; the clones are recruited via a #clearrec rebuild. Two-form mermen are edited in both forms. #copystats overwrites everything (incl. weapons/armour), so it is placed first and weapons are re-set with #clearweapons.

Recruitment rebuild

After #clearrec, the roster is rebuilt to mirror the vanilla fort/coast split plus the land theme, grouped by family (troops weak→strong; commanders command→priest→mage):

  • Underwater home fort (#uwrec/#uwcom): Knife Militia, Turtle Clan, Pelagian Soldier, Coastal Warrior, Merman Hunter, Coral Clan Soldier, Shark Tribe Berserker, Triton Rider; Scout, Captain, Merman Chief, Merman Priest, Pearl Clan Priest, Mermage, Pearl Mage.
  • Coastal forts (#coastfortrec/#coastfortcom): Coastal Warrior, Shore Fighter, Sideraspist, Vanguard Warrior; Merman Chief, Merman Priest, Shore Commander, Explorer, Vanguard Captain, Ichtyid Pearl Mage.
  • Land forts — Closed Realm (#landrec/#landcom): Coastal Warrior, Shore Fighter, Sideraspist, Vanguard Warrior, Closed Realm Soldier; Merman Chief, Merman Priest, Shore Commander, Explorer, Vanguard Captain, Closed Realm Captain.

Spear Militia (1056) is dropped. Capital-only units (Pearl King 2397, Pearl Guard 2387, Ichtyid Pearl Mage 2805) come from the Palace of Pearls site, not the recruit list, so they are not re-added — Ichtyid Pearl Mage additionally gets coastal recruitment per the brain dump.

Open questions for the author

1. Mermage random — Air or Astral? "A random" is implemented as Air (256), matching the Pearl Mage's W/A/S/N. Change to Astral (2048) if that was meant.
2. Shark Tribe Berserker rp cost. Brain dump said "increase rp cost"; gold is 13 but no rp target was given, so #rpcost is left at the copystats default. Provide a value and it's a one-liner.
3. Triton Rider "reduce rp cost." Not actionable — its resource cost is already 1. Did you mean gold, or a per-turn recruit limit? Left unchanged.
4. Astral Suppressor target cost. #nationrebate 40 applies the standard national rebate to item 895 (currently 3 astral gems in DE). Confirm the resulting cost is low enough for the Pearl King.
5. Laistrygones popkill. Set to 2 per the brain dump; note the base was already 1, so this is a slight increase. Confirm direction.
6. Word of Drowning / Choking Brine are #restricted to ~12 underwater nations, so the range/precision buffs apply to all of them. Confirm that's intended (else they need EA-only clones).
7. Masters of the Sea (3069) — left as-is, awaiting a concrete spec.
